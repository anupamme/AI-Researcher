\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Neural embedding of symbolic-expression equivalence classes.} The line this paper audits begins with \emph{neural equivalence networks} \citep{allamanis2017learning}, which learn continuous representations of boolean and polynomial expressions trained to place semantically equivalent but syntactically dissimilar expressions together, and evaluate on held-out equivalence classes (\textsc{UnseenEqClass}). \citet{gangwar2023semantic} take a sequence-to-sequence approach over equivalent transcendental and algebraic pairs, and \citet{zheng2025egen} generate training pairs from e-graphs. All three define equivalence classes over a \emph{shared} variable pool, which, as our survey shows (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark_survey}), is exactly the design property that makes them immune to the variable-identity shortcut we document. Our EQNET audit (\S\ref{sec:causal_external}) is a direct evaluation on that benchmark family.

\paragraph{Shortcut learning, annotation artifacts, and control tasks.} The method here instantiates a well-established methodology. \citet{geirhos2020shortcut} frame \emph{shortcut learning} (models exploiting cues that satisfy the objective without capturing the intended concept) as a pervasive failure mode. In NLI, \citet{gururangan2018annotation} and \citet{poliak2018hypothesis} showed hypothesis-only baselines could match full models, revealing artifacts rather than reasoning; our zero-parameter bag-of-tokens classifier is the symbolic analogue, and on the AI~Feynman suite it \emph{exceeds} the trained encoder (Table~\ref{tab:held_out_form}). \citet{hewitt2019control} introduced \emph{control tasks}: probing accuracy is interpretable only against a randomized baseline, which is what our random-encoder skyline supplies. What is new is the tier decomposition of \S\ref{sec:tiers}, the renaming-control taxonomy that isolates each tier, and the targeted audit (across 23 corpora from four families) of which benchmark families are exposed at which tier. Renaming-invariance has an analogue in code: Type-2 clones are by definition identical up to identifier names, and clone benchmarks curate them as a distinct category \citep{svajlenko2014towards}; related work learns representations over ASTs and clone graphs \citep{zhang2019novel, wang2020detecting}.

\paragraph{Held-out construction and evaluation contamination.} SCAN \citep{lake2018generalization} and COGS \citep{kim2020cogs} established that systematic generalisation is not guaranteed by standard splits and that \emph{held-out construction} determines observed difficulty. Our matched contrast (Table~\ref{tab:diversity_sweep}) is the symbolic-expression analogue: the held-out form plays the role of the test composition, and how it is built decides whether it is genuinely out of distribution. The contamination literature \citep{jacovi2023stop, zhou2023don} likewise documents benchmark performance achieved by surface pattern matching and recommends shortcut-breaking held-out sets; our checklist operationalises that recommendation, with the screening step shipped as a tool.

\paragraph{Encoders, metric learning, and physics-informed symbolic models.} We use Tree-LSTMs \citep{tai2015improved}, GINs \citep{xu2019powerful}, and Transformers over linearised trees \citep{vaswani2017attention, lample2019deep} with a semi-hard triplet objective \citep{schroff2015facenet}, in the tradition of per-class anchor methods \citep{wen2016discriminative, snell2017prototypical} and of the observation that fixed random classifiers can match learned ones when geometry is regularised \citep{hoffer2018fix}. The last point recurs here: wherever equation identity is recoverable by another route, anchor provenance stops mattering, which is the condition a held-out-form protocol must rule out before crediting an encoder. On the symbolic side, \citet{xie2019embedding} embed propositional formulae via satisfaction supervision, and symbolic-regression systems \citep{udrescu2020ai, biggio2021neural, cranmer2019learning, stewart2017label} recover expressions from data; two of these corpora \citep{udrescu2020ai, lample2019deep} supply the benchmarks we audit. All three of our architectures show the same variable-identity leakage under renaming, which locates the mechanism in the benchmark design rather than the encoder.
