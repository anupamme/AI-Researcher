\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\textbf{We establish---(1)--(3) structurally or by replication, (4)--(5) as a weaker, target-dependent secondary finding:} \textbf{(1)}~instructed-roleplay designs identify the \emph{instruction}'s effect and cannot bound \emph{deception}'s---an \emph{identification} failure, not a detector failure; \textbf{(2)}~the confound is \emph{active}, not hypothetical, across \emph{three} detector paradigms, the score moving inside a single deception label; \textbf{(3)}~across \emph{both} public rollout releases, nine of ten rollout sets cannot express the criterion that removes the confound on a deployed-observable channel, and the closest fail three ways: an instrument-specific null, \textbf{annotation-channel leakage}, and \textbf{construct recovery}; and \textbf{(4)}~on materials we built to satisfy §1's (i)--(v), prior work's own battery \emph{does} separate operationally verified deception on three of five targets with power, and after a blinded re-run plus one of five \emph{new} targets, \textbf{a pre-registered covariate audit withdraws one, leaving one of five standing} (EXP-C4B/AE); and \textbf{(5)}~\textbf{the audit changes \emph{which} behavior indicates deception}---the instructed benchmark's best probe is inert at fixed $E$, another replacing it (EXP-AD). \textbf{The contribution is an operational identification audit for behavioral LLM benchmarks, together with empirical evidence that the common instructed-lie paradigm produces large, reproducible attribution failures across three detector paradigms and that benchmark design can determine which behavioral signal appears to constitute ``deception detection''.}

\textbf{We do not establish} the five negatives boxed in §\ref{sec:eval_controls} (summarized in §1). The alternative reading---that deception is undetectable---is what (4) tests, and the evidence runs against it. The general lesson, which we name the \textbf{Elicitation--Construct Confounding Principle}, is that a benchmark label generated by an intervention can quietly become a label for the intervention itself. \textbf{Its precondition is decidable outside deception and we decide it}---on four published designs and 28 pinned corpora---\textbf{while its magnitude we measure only for behavioral deception detection} (EXP-AF, Appendix~\ref{app:eccp_decidability}).
