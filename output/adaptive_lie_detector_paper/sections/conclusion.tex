\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

High behavioral lie-detection accuracy in instructed LLM roleplay does not establish deception-specific detection. A from-scratch reimplementation of \citet{pacchiardi2023catch}'s mechanism falls to chance under equalization on four of five targets; our pipeline collapses 30--41\,pp. The factorial (§\ref{sec:factorial}) shows a large, consistent response to the instruction and a sign-changing response to claim content --- but since the instruction also produces the deception, the benchmark cannot separate them. That is an \emph{identification} failure, not a demonstrated detector failure. Generalization is weak (64.2\%), and a lexical regex reaching 69--80\% brackets the pipeline.
