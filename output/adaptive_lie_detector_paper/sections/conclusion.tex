\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

High behavioral lie-detection accuracy in instructed LLM roleplay does not establish deception-specific detection: the instruction is what produces the deception, so this is an \emph{identification} failure rather than a detector failure. Of ten published rollout sets, nine cannot express the criterion that could attribute a signal to deception, and on the tenth the rule finds no evidence of one in the deployed-observable channel. Before investing in higher detector accuracy, the field needs benchmarks that vary deception at fixed elicitation, grade it independently of both the condition label and the detector's own input, observe the same item under both conditions, and expose only the channel a deployed detector reads.
