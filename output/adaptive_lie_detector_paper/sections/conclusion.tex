\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\textbf{We establish:} \textbf{(1)}~instructed-roleplay designs identify the effect of the \emph{instruction} and cannot bound that of \emph{deception}---an \emph{identification} failure, not a detector failure, and one no gain in accuracy, scale or sophistication repairs; \textbf{(2)}~the confound is \emph{active} in this paradigm, not hypothetical, and it holds across \emph{three} detector paradigms---two unrelated behavioral detectors collapse under one control, a parameter-free surface rule reproduces most of the benchmark-level accuracy with no deception-specific modelling, and a white-box activation probe, at ceiling on the instructed contrast in two model families, transfers at chance---while the score moves inside a single deception label; \textbf{(3)}~across \emph{both} public rollout releases---ten rollout sets, and 35 further cells over five generator families---nine of the ten cannot express the one criterion that removes the elicitation confound on a deployed-observable channel; on the tenth our surface rule is null while prior work's own judge separates deception, so the null is instrument-specific; on the \emph{hidden} channel the same rule looks decisive---\textbf{annotation-channel leakage}; and on the second release, where the paired scenarios finally exist, the label still grades the very text both instruments read---\textbf{construct recovery}; and \textbf{(4)}~on materials we built to satisfy all of §1's (i)--(v)---one fixed system prompt per claim, no lie instruction, $D$ graded from a channel the detector never reads---prior work's own battery \emph{does} separate realized deception on three of five targets---all three in which the design had power ($p\!\leq\!0.006$; two underpowered nulls, MDE $\ge\!0.80$); \textbf{a blinded 8--10-target replication is the natural next step, and is not claimed here}. \textbf{The canonical instructed benchmark is therefore non-identifying, and deception is nevertheless detectable under a valid fixed-elicitation design}---deception-\emph{associated}, not $\tau_D$.

\textbf{We do not establish} that published detectors read only instruction-following, that equalization proves no signal is present, or that the audited corpora contain no deception. \textbf{The strongest alternative interpretation---that our critique merely reflects an inability to detect deception---is the one (4) tests, and it does not hold}: (1)--(3) survive it as claims about what \emph{instructed} benchmarks identify and what public rollout corpora contain today. \textbf{The general lesson is that a behavioral benchmark label generated by an intervention can quietly become a label for the intervention itself}, which is checkable by whoever did not build the benchmark.
