\textbf{The structural result and the survey result are not to be conflated, and neither denies Q1}: that instructed designs identify $\tau_E$ and cannot bound $\tau_D$ holds by the design's structure, whereas what public corpora supply today is a \emph{survey} finding. Only correction-marker density clears reliability (Krippendorff's $\alpha\!=\!0.606$, vs.\ 0.070--0.264 for the other four), and the extractor-independent claims are unaffected; transfer beyond instructed roleplay is \textbf{exploratory}, null or near-null with the system prompt controlled. Leave-one-model-out yields \textbf{64.2\%} [59--69\%]: the \emph{collapse} replicates across three paradigms and two claim sets, while the \emph{residual signal} does not transfer---as a robust deception representation would. \textbf{EXP-WP is one model of one size class on two claim sets we built ourselves}, and its steering arm is null, so the $E$ direction is \textbf{decodable but not causal}. \textbf{Our attempted fixed-$D$ intervention was empirically infeasible at useful scale under this model class} (EXP-IB null); criterion 4 is testable only externally, and only on ten sets from one release, every rollout Llama-70B. Targets are \textbf{English-language instructed roleplay on instruction-tuned open-weight models, 3B--70B}, with autonomous strategic deception~\citep{hubinger2019risks, scheurer2024strategic, greenblatt2024alignment} entering only through §\ref{sec:external_audit}'s audit of corpora we did not build (Appendices~\ref{app:probe_audit},~\ref{app:intervention_b},~\ref{app:loo_across_models},~\ref{app:limitations_detail}).
