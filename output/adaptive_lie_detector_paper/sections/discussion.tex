\textbf{The structural result and the survey result are not to be conflated, and neither denies Q1}: the first holds by design, the second is a \emph{survey} of what public corpora supply today. Only correction-marker density clears reliability (Krippendorff's $\alpha\!=\!0.606$, vs.\ 0.070--0.264 for the other four), the extractor-independent claims being unaffected; transfer beyond instructed roleplay is \textbf{exploratory}. The \emph{collapse} replicates across three paradigms and two claim sets; the \emph{residual signal} does not transfer across held-out targets. \textbf{EXP-WP is two 4B models in two families}, its steering arm null in both (§\ref{sec:td_vs_fd}). \textbf{Our fixed-$D$ intervention was infeasible at useful scale here} (EXP-IB null); on public corpora the two releases are independent in \emph{generator} but not in \emph{environment}---the one cell satisfying (i)--(iv) re-generates the same insider-trading scenario. \textbf{EXP-C4 meets criterion~4 on materials we built} (3 of 5 positive; \textbf{2 of 3 replicate} blinded, \textbf{1 of 5} new targets, EXP-C4B) but is \emph{stratified observational} at fixed $E$, so a latent $U$ driving both $D$ and $S$ survives it. Targets are \textbf{English-language instructed roleplay on instruction-tuned open-weight models, 3B--70B}; autonomous strategic deception~\citep{hubinger2019risks, scheurer2024strategic, greenblatt2024alignment} enters only through §\ref{sec:external_audit} (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations_detail}).
