\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Human Validation of the Behavioral Features}
\label{sec:icc_caveat}
\label{sec:icc_owned}

Four of five behavioral features fail human inter-rater reliability: the $n\!=\!100$/3-annotator study (Appendix~\ref{app:human_icc_n100}) gives $\alpha\!=\!0.606$ for correction-marker density but 0.070--0.264 for the rest. We claim \emph{reliability} only, not construct validity: $\alpha$ shows annotators agree with one another, not that the feature measures deception --- and the construct-overlap evidence is weaker still (extractor vs.\ human ratings, Pearson $r\!=\!0.360$). Restricting the pipeline to the one feature that clears reliability moves accuracy from 64.7\% to \textbf{54.5\%} (Appendix~\ref{app:feature_validity}); we let that comparison stand rather than attributing a share of the signal. The L-indep claims (the rule, the 30--41\,pp collapse) are unaffected.

Two frontier targets fail in opposite ways --- marker suppression on Claude Sonnet~4.5, classifier-transfer failure on Llama~4~Maverick --- but at $n\!=\!2$ with a same-family confound neither is interpretable (Appendix~\ref{app:frontier_preliminary}).

\subsection{Summary of Contributions}
\label{sec:novelty}

The contribution is the \textbf{causal evaluation protocol}: three diagnostics attribute reported accuracy to instruction-following (30--41\,pp), extractor quality ($+$7--10\,pp), and surface-lexical patterns, with the factorial (§\ref{sec:factorial}) supplying the causal test. Applied to prior work's own detector, the conclusion is not contingent on our pipeline --- the same collapse appears in the mechanism of \citet{pacchiardi2023catch} and in white-box SAPLMA-style probes (§\ref{sec:saplma_application}).

\paragraph{Generalization, false positives, and limitations.}
\label{sec:loo_across_models}
\label{sec:limitations}
\label{sec:interrogation_role}
\label{sec:deployment}
Leave-one-model-out yields \textbf{64.2\%} [CI: 59--69\%], within 3.0\,pp of pooled LOO (67.1\%); leave-one-claim-pair-out yields \textbf{67.4\%}, matching trial LOO to 0.3\,pp and ruling out claim-level leakage. The rule fired once in 300 benign trials (0.3\%). Targets are instruction-tuned $\leq$70B; frontier and multilingual/autonomous regimes are open (Appendices~\ref{app:loo_across_models},~\ref{app:limitations_detail},~\ref{app:future_directions}).
