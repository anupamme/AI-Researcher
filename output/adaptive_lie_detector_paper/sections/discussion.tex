Leave-one-model-out yields \textbf{64.2\%} [59--69\%]. Two claims must not be conflated: the \emph{collapse} replicates across two detectors, two claim sets and two probe banks and is the paper's general finding, whereas the \emph{residual signal} does not transfer across models. \textbf{That non-transfer supports the thesis rather than undercutting it}: a robust deception representation would transfer; a model-specific behavioral residual is what the identification argument predicts is left over. \textbf{Intervention (B) remains unsupplied} (EXP-IB is null on its primary target and could not be built at scale, Appendix~\ref{app:intervention_b}), and criterion 4 is testable only externally, where the one corpus that can express it returns a null, so \textbf{no design reported here attributes the signal to deception}---the paper's claim rather than a gap in it. Targets are instruction-tuned $\leq$70B; larger models and multilingual regimes are open (Appendices~\ref{app:loo_across_models},~\ref{app:limitations_detail}).
