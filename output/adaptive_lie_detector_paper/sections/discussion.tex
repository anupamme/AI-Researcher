Only correction-marker density clears reliability (Krippendorff's $\alpha\!=\!0.606$, vs.\ 0.070--0.264 for the other four); \textbf{human rating is not a premise of the identification result}---the equalization experiments (EXP-R1/R1b/R1c) and the lexical audit use neither LLM-extracted features nor human ratings; transfer beyond instructed roleplay is exploratory. The \emph{collapse} replicates across three paradigms and two claim sets; the \emph{residual signal} does not transfer across held-out targets. The white-box probe audit (EXP-WP) is two 4B models in two families, its steering arm null in both (§\ref{sec:td_vs_fd}). \textbf{Our fixed-$D$ intervention was infeasible at useful scale here} (EXP-IB null); on public corpora the two releases are independent in \emph{generator} but not in \emph{environment}---the one cell satisfying (i)--(iv) re-generates the same insider-trading scenario. \textbf{The criterion-4 contrast (EXP-C4) meets criterion~4 on materials we built} (positive on \textbf{1 of 5} new targets, EXP-C4B) but is \emph{stratified observational} at fixed $E$, so a latent $U$ driving both $D$ and $S$ survives it. Targets are \textbf{English-language instructed roleplay on instruction-tuned open-weight models, 3B--70B}; autonomous strategic deception~\citep{hubinger2019risks, scheurer2024strategic, greenblatt2024alignment} enters only through §\ref{sec:external_audit} (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations_detail}).
