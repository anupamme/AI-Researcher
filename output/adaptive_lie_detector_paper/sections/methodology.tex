\subsection{Q2: Why the Instructed Contrast Cannot Be Attributed}
\label{sec:identification}


\textbf{An instructed-lie benchmark changes two things with one edit.} Telling a model to lie makes it deceive, and it also makes it comply with an assigned communicative goal: a different task, differently worded, differently performed. A detector scored on that contrast records that the output moved, not \emph{which} of the two moved it.

Write $E$ for the deception instruction, $C$ for compliance behavior, and $S$ for the detector's output. Deception is the \emph{act}: \textbf{$D\!=\!1$ iff the target asserts $p$ while holding $\neg p$}. It is binary, latent, and observed by no benchmark audited here. Deception, compliance, the claim's truth-value and the target's belief are all parents of $S$ (Figure~\ref{fig:dag}; §\ref{sec:belief_strata}). Instructed benchmarks label trials by the claim's truth-value and by the instruction, never by the target's belief and never by $D$, so \textbf{the ground-truth label is an experimental condition, not an observation of latent intent} (Appendix~\ref{app:criteria_taxonomy}).

\paragraph{Two estimands.} A benchmark of this kind measures the \emph{instruction}'s effect on the detector; the claim it is read as supporting is about \emph{deception}'s. These are different quantities:
\begin{equation*}
\tau_E \;=\; \mathbb{E}[S \mid \mathrm{do}(E{=}1)] - \mathbb{E}[S \mid \mathrm{do}(E{=}0)],
\qquad \tau_D \;\text{likewise, with } D \text{ in place of } E,
\end{equation*}
and the benchmark contrast is generated by intervention on $E$, so \textbf{classification accuracy is evidence about the resulting $E$-associated separation, not directly about $D$}. $\tau_E$ is the estimand that separation belongs to, not a quantity the score measures. \textbf{$\tau_D$ is the estimand the claim is about, not one we estimate}: $D$ is latent and not directly settable, so the strongest thing an audit can ask for is the weaker quantity criterion~4 reaches, a deception-\emph{associated} signal at fixed elicitation (§\ref{sec:eval_controls}). \textbf{Nothing below requires $\mathrm{do}(D)$ to be well defined}: the result is about which pathways an intervention on $E$ can separate.

\paragraph{The Elicitation--Construct Confounding Principle (ECCP).} \emph{When a benchmark creates its target construct $Y$ by an intervention $E$ that also changes the elicited behavior $C$ a detector reads, no score evaluated only under $\mathrm{do}(E)$ is attributable to $Y$.} \textbf{The ECCP names a benchmark-design instance of standard pathway non-identifiability, not a new theoretical result}: its premises~(A1)--(A5) are stated in full in Appendix~\ref{app:criteria_taxonomy}, and \textbf{what is new is the audit it licenses}, not the constraint. The Proposition below is that statement for $k$ unobserved mechanisms; \textbf{behavioral deception detection is the instantiation $(M_1,M_2)=(D,C)$}. \textbf{It also names an escape route a designer controls, and public corpora already take it piecewise}: of \textbf{28} pinned public corpora, \textbf{2, 21 and 3} supply each of the three properties that make a benchmark auditable on $Y$ (crossing elicitation with items, recording the channel a detector reads, grading $Y$ off that channel), and \textbf{none supplies all three}, so an auditable benchmark needs no new idea, only the combination. \textbf{Outside deception the precondition is thus decidable and decided; the magnitude is measured only for deception} (EXP-AF, Appendix~\ref{app:eccp_decidability}).

\noindent In the statement below, $V$ is the claim's truth-value, so $(V,E,S)$ is what an instructed benchmark records.

\paragraph{Proposition (pathway non-separability).} \emph{Let $M_1,\dots,M_k$ be latent mechanisms that $\mathrm{do}(E)$ shifts, none of them observed. Restrict interventions to $\mathrm{do}(E)$ and observations to $(V,E,S)$. Then no function of the observed distribution identifies any individual $M_i$'s contribution to $S$, \textbf{for any causal arrangement among the $M_i$ consistent with all of them being descendants of $E$}. With $(M_1,M_2)=(D,C)$: $\tau_E$ neither bounds nor signs $\tau_D$, the total effect redistributing between the two paths in any proportion, up to $\tau_D\!=\!0$, without changing the distribution over $(V,E,S)$.}

\paragraph{Corollary (scale does not help).} \emph{No estimator that is a function of interventions on $E$ and observations of $(V,E,S)$ identifies $\tau_D$, however precisely $\tau_E$ is estimated.} No increase in accuracy, model scale, or detector sophistication resolves this unless the evaluation introduces an intervention that separates deception from instruction-following. Nor is the distinction semantic: instruction-following explains instructed-benchmark accuracy and nothing outside it, while uninstructed deception has no instruction to follow, and evaluations there report the predicted drop~\citep{cooney2026didyoulie}.

\paragraph{What would license an attribution to $\tau_D$.} Two designs stand in for $\mathrm{do}(D)$, and \textbf{neither is it}. \textbf{(A)}, our criterion 4 (§\ref{sec:eval_controls}): vary $D$ with $E$ fixed, uninstructed deception under incentive or goal conflict, \emph{observational at fixed $E$} (rung~4 of Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}). \textbf{(B)}~vary $E$ with $D$ fixed, so that compliance is sincere. \textbf{We attempt both}: (B) proved \emph{empirically infeasible at useful scale} (pre-registered null: EXP-IB, Appendix~\ref{app:intervention_b}); (A) we built ourselves (EXP-C4, §\ref{sec:external_audit}), criterion~4 met, still not $\mathrm{do}(D)$.

\paragraph{How the criteria divide: A (1,2,3) + B (4) + C (5).} \textbf{The three groups differ in kind}: \textbf{1--3 diagnose the confound}, \textbf{criterion~4 is the one \emph{identification} requirement}, and \textbf{criterion~5 is \emph{robustness}, not a construct-validity test}; criterion~1 is how the confound is \emph{detected}, not a condition of validity. The division is \emph{who can apply} a criterion: \textbf{A and C can be checked on someone else's benchmark, from the artifact as released; B requires building materials that no instructed design can supply}. \textbf{Criterion~4 does not validate deception detection; it tests whether the benchmark has crossed the minimum design boundary at which deception attribution becomes meaningful.} \textbf{Criteria 1--5 and requirements~(i)--(v) are different lists}: the criteria are the audit's controls, (i)--(v) operationalize criterion~4. \textbf{Two senses of ``necessary'', kept apart}: (i)~$E$ fixed and (ii)~$D$ graded independently of the condition label are \textbf{necessary for identification}; (iii)--(v) are \textbf{necessary for this audit to be operational}: (iii)~is how an auditor \emph{checks} that no observable lies on one pathway alone, and (v)~rules out definitional circularity, a threat to the \emph{claim} rather than to the identification (Appendix~\ref{app:criteria_taxonomy}). \textbf{The five are not claimed to characterize every valid deception benchmark}: they rule out specific alternative explanations. \textbf{Passing criterion~4 is necessary, not sufficient}: at fixed $E$ deception varies \emph{naturally}, so any latent $U$ with $U\!\to\!D$ and $U\!\to\!S$ survives it; criterion~4 removes the \emph{elicitation} confound and no other, and what it establishes is deception-associated behavior \emph{conditional on the operational belief criterion}. The output is a verdict on a benchmark, not a detector: \textbf{construct validity and instrument validity are distinct}, and criterion~4 tests only the first; \textbf{the protocol falsifies validity claims rather than certifying them}. Our own instructed materials fail 1, 3 and 5; the materials we built (EXP-C4) supply criterion~4 by construction.

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.975\linewidth}{\small\textbf{What is proven, what is shown at fixed elicitation, and what is unresolved.} \textbf{Load-bearing chain}: identification~§\ref{sec:identification} $\to$ equalization~§\ref{sec:r1_faithful} $\to$ surface~§\ref{sec:hedging_baseline} $\to$ white-box~§\ref{sec:whitebox} $\to$ public corpora and criterion~4~§\ref{sec:external_audit}; the rest is appendix.
\textbf{Inferential unit: target~$\times$~claim-pair.} The primary evidence is replicated across target~$\times$~claim-pair groups; \textbf{individual trial counts are not treated as independent evidence of a population-level effect}: the thousands of graded trials reduce to \textbf{12--23 paired claims per target} (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:inference_ladder}).
\textbf{Establishes.} \textbf{(a)}~Instructed-lie accuracy cannot attribute itself to $D$ (by structure). \textbf{(b)}~The confound is \emph{active}, across three detector paradigms. \textbf{(c)}~Much of the accuracy is reachable from surface behavior. \textbf{(d)}~No audited public release supplies all five requirements. \textbf{(e)}~A fixed-elicitation design \emph{does} yield a deception-\emph{associated} signal: \textbf{rung~4 of Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}, not rung~5}. \textbf{The two halves are not equally strong}: (a)--(d) hold by structure or replicate across paradigms, claim sets and model families, while \textbf{(e) is target-dependent} and the weakest thing here (\textbf{one} of five standing after a pre-registered covariate audit withdrew one, one of five new) on 3B--14B open-weight targets.
\textbf{Does not establish.} \textbf{(1)}~Anything against Q1, or that published detectors measure only instruction-following: we audit what their evaluations attribute, not what their features encode. \textbf{(2)}~That equalization establishes the \emph{absence} of a signal: it may remove the deception with the confound (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:roadmap}). \textbf{(3)}~That the residual is entirely lexical: a rule \emph{reaching} benchmark accuracy shows what is surface-reachable without decomposing the detector's signal. \textbf{(4)}~That the audited rollout sets contain no deception: none \emph{can attribute} one on a deployed-observable channel, a property of the designs, not the models. \textbf{(5)}~That the fixed-elicitation signal is deception \emph{rather than} a correlate of it: uncertainty, refusal, conflict resolution, claim difficulty, length or style all vary with $D$ at fixed $E$; the covariate audit (EXP-AE) gives them the battery's own estimator, and \textbf{five of ten stay live, one positive is withdrawn, and the battery is not shown to add over them}. (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:what_establishes}.)

\textbf{Unresolved.} Whether $D$ \emph{causes} $S$. Criterion~4 is \textbf{the minimum observational contrast needed to remove the specific elicitation confound}, and no more: it delivers $D_{\mathrm{operational}}\!\leftrightarrow\!S$ at fixed $E$ (an association at byte-identical elicitation), so any latent $U$ with $U\!\to\!D$ and $U\!\to\!S$ survives it. $D\!\to\!S$ requires $D$ \emph{manipulated}, which is rung~5, and \textbf{no design audited here, ours included, reaches it}.}}
