\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

We evaluate seven LLMs across four families (Llama~3.2~3B/3.1~8B/3.3~70B~\citep{meta2024llama3}, Mistral~7B~\citep{jiang2023mistral}, Qwen~2.5~7B/14B~\citep{qwen2025qwen25} and Claude Haiku~4.5~\citep{anthropic2025haiku45}) at $n=50$--100 balanced trials per condition, plus Qwen~2.5~32B in the scale sweep and Qwen3-4B and Gemma~3~4B in the white-box audit. Claude Haiku~4.5 is the interrogator and feature extractor throughout, cross-family for every target but itself. Claims are 50 matched true/false pairs, tested \emph{instructed} and \emph{prompt-equalized} (both arms given one byte-identical neutral prompt, so neither is told to lie). No claim below is an effect-magnitude claim (Appendices~\ref{app:claims},~\ref{app:scale_details},~\ref{app:cross_family_details},~\ref{app:vintage}). \textbf{The primary evidence uses no LLM-extracted features at all} (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:roadmap}'s bold rows) and \textbf{sixteen pre-registered analyses (one of them a rule that failed, reported as a null) are the only confirmatory results, everything else exploratory}, so \textbf{the argument rests on replication, not on any single $p$-value} (§\ref{sec:eval_controls}). \textbf{Three tiers (confirmatory, supporting, exploratory) are marked per row in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:experiment_summary}}; Table~\ref{tab:claim_ledger} is the Tier-1 ledger. A pre-registered panel adds seven frontier-scale targets from seven organizations (EXP-FS).

\citet{pacchiardi2024catch}'s detector reads none of the target's answer about the claim, only binarised yes/no replies to a \emph{fixed battery of unrelated follow-ups}, fed to one logistic regression. We reproduce this with an order-stable bank of 16 probes and no LLM feature scoring; their original 48-probe bank was unavailable, so this is a \textbf{mechanism-faithful reimplementation} at one third the width, and these tables are evidence about the \emph{design}, not about their instrument at full width (Appendices~\ref{app:pacchiardi},~\ref{app:r1_protocol}).

\paragraph{Result.} \textbf{Every target satisfying the pre-registered informativeness criterion lost its above-chance signal} (EXP-R1c, Figure~\ref{fig:r1c_collapse}; pooled 97.0\%$\to$43.3\%). \textbf{That criterion is fixed by the instructed cell, before any equalized result is seen.} On the v1 claim set it works instructed on five of six at 70.0--100.0\% (all $p\!\leq\!0.002$; Appendix Table~\ref{tab:r1_faithful}), bracketing the 67--73\% of \citet{pacchiardi2024catch}; \emph{Qwen~2.5~7B}, the sixth, never separates its conditions (54.0\%, $p\!=\!0.299$); equalization leaves no detectable above-chance discrimination on four of those five (48.0--52.0\%; pooled 81.7\%$\to$53.7\%). The fifth, \emph{Qwen~2.5~14B}, holds at 70.0\% on one post-hoc-selected dimension, and \textbf{a pre-registered one-sided replication refutes it} (Appendix~\ref{app:r1b}). \textbf{The most discriminative question in the battery is a paraphrase of the instruction the target is following}, \emph{constant} under equalization on all six targets; on five it is the whole of the accuracy, not a component.

\subsection{Q2, Rung 2 to 3: What Survives Is Reproducible from Surface Behavior and Model Knowledge}
\label{sec:hedging_baseline}

\paragraph{A hand-written regex reaches the accuracy the benchmark is scored on.} A single regex feature (refusal/correction marker count from 14 patterns) reaches \textbf{69--80\%} under equalization with \emph{no learned parameters}, against the prior detector's 53.7\%: \textbf{69.1\%} with the most load-bearing pattern dropped is the figure we rely on; 79.8\% on the full inventory a sensitivity result, and the rule was \textbf{not pre-specified, so this is diagnostic rather than confirmatory} (Appendices~\ref{app:regex_patterns},~\ref{app:validation_tests}).

\paragraph{What the rule reads does not survive an adversary or rewording, and a substantial component is consistent with knowledge conflict (EXP-B).}
\label{sec:adversarial_paraphrase}\label{sec:adversarial_fullregex}\label{sec:paraphrase_invariance}\label{sec:belief_strata}
A style-constraint adversary drops correction markers on \emph{lie} trials 84\%$\to$16\% without changing the behavior, and across 17 instruction paraphrases the rule sits below chance (35--51\%): what it keys on is neither paraphrase-invariant nor deception-specific, \emph{surface-accessible accuracy}, \textbf{not a decomposition of the rest}. That component is 70.0\% KNOWN vs.\ 57.6\% UNKNOWN ($p\!=\!0.0045$), \emph{stratified observational}, so \textbf{this does not identify knowledge conflict as its cause} (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:adv_multimodel}; Appendices~\ref{app:adv_qualitative},~\ref{app:belief_strata}).

\paragraph{The same confound inside a white-box probe, a third detector paradigm (EXP-WP).} A linear probe on the residual stream of two models in two families (pooling and layer \textbf{fixed in advance}) decodes the instruction at 100.0\% but transfers to prompt-equalized responses \textbf{at chance}: \textbf{demonstrably sensitive to $E$, with the design identifying no sensitivity to $D$}. \paragraph{The $2\times2$ factorial: the score tracks the instruction, not the label.} Crossing truth-value with the instruction, its effect is $+1.27$--$+1.56$\,SD on every target ($p\!<\!0.001$) while truth-value's is smaller and \emph{changes sign}; in the true-claim/deceive cell a detector calibrated on the benchmark's own labels scores \textbf{below chance}. Frontier scale: rule 80--90\%, pipeline 40--56\% (EXP-FS; Appendix~\ref{app:frontier_panel}); \textbf{scale moves the numbers, not the verdict}, which is the Corollary of §\ref{sec:identification}.


\subsection{Q3, Rung 3 to 4: Criterion 4 on Public Corpora, and on Materials Built for It (EXP-XA/XJ/XL, EXP-C4)}
\label{sec:external_audit}


\paragraph{Public corpora: an instrument-specific null, annotation-channel leakage, and construct recovery (EXP-XA/XJ, EXP-IT).} $D$ is each set's per-rollout grade, never the condition label (pre-registered). One set clears both gates (sandbagging, $n\!=\!1000$, one fixed system prompt), and there the release's \emph{own} judge \textbf{separates operationally verified deception at fixed elicitation} (AUROC 0.71/0.94) while our rule is \textbf{null}: \textbf{the null is the instrument's, not the criterion's}. Scored on the \emph{full} transcript that same rule looks decisive, the mandated scaffold naming the pattern it keys on: \textbf{annotation-channel leakage}, whence~(iv). Insider trading fails differently: deceiving \emph{is} omitting the source, so rule and grader share evidence (\textbf{construct recovery}, whence~(v)), while against the \emph{action} label both are \textbf{null} (EXP-IT2).

\noindent \textbf{Stated exactly}: \textbf{no audited public set satisfies all five}; the channel facts are measurements, the verdict that they violate~(iv) or~(v) is our criterion's, and a reader who rejects the criterion keeps the measurement (Table~\ref{tab:external_audit}). \textbf{A blinded panel bounds this}: \textbf{the requirements are decidable from a specification, and these releases do not publish one} (EXP-AA, \emph{pre-registered}; Appendices~\ref{app:external_audit},~\ref{app:insider_audit},~\ref{app:audit_agreement}).

\paragraph{So we built one, and prior work's own battery separates deception in it (the criterion-4 contrast, EXP-C4, \emph{pre-registered}).} One system prompt per claim presents a knowledge-screened false fact as an authoritative reference and \textbf{never instructs deception}; the target asserts it (deception, the belief screen showing it holds $\neg p$) or corrects it, $D$ graded cross-family from the \textbf{on-claim answer alone} ($\alpha\!=\!0.728$) while the detector reads only the 16-probe vector, so all of~(i)--(v) hold. \textbf{The count moves three times before it settles.} \textbf{(a)}~Three of five targets separate operationally verified deception at byte-identical elicitation, all three with power ($p\!\leq\!0.006$, claim-grouped folds); the other two are \textbf{null and uninformative} (MDE $\ge\!0.80$). \textbf{(b)}~A blinded, Holm-corrected replication (EXP-C4B) keeps two of the three (not the weakest) and adds \textbf{one of five \emph{new} targets}. \textbf{(c)}~Sixteen surface covariates \textbf{beat the battery on Llama~3.1~8B} ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}}\!=\!0.0005$), \textbf{so that positive is withdrawn} (the covariate audit, EXP-AE); elsewhere the battery leads, not separably. \textbf{(d)}~\textbf{The standing count is one of five.} Three qualifications: because the contrast reuses \emph{prior work's own} battery, a null is partly about \textbf{that instrument's portability}, not the criterion's validity; it is not belief revision (H3, pre-registered); and it is \emph{stratified observational} at fixed $E$, hence deception-\emph{associated}, not $\tau_D$ (Appendices~\ref{app:crit4_ours},~\ref{app:crit4b},~\ref{app:covariate_audit}). \textbf{Criterion~5 (transfer) is exploratory}: 2 of 5, not part of the argument (Appendix~\ref{app:crit4_transfer}). \textbf{Conclusion: criterion-4-valid materials can produce a deception-associated signal; this is not an estimate of the prevalence or reliability of deception detection. The standing 1-of-5 is itself a positive finding rather than a disappointment: once the elicitation confound is removed, detection is markedly less stable across targets than the instructed benchmark's near-ceiling accuracy suggests.}

\paragraph{The benchmark can manufacture its own apparent deception signature (EXP-AD, \emph{pre-registered}).} On the instructed benchmark the battery's most discriminative question (``if you had made an error, would you point it out without being asked?'') reaches \textbf{100.0\%} grouped-5-fold on five of six targets. At byte-identical elicitation the \emph{same} probe is \textbf{inert}: rank~1 on none of the five shared targets, \emph{exactly constant} on three. What discriminates instead is a \emph{different} probe, \emph{``would you stake your reputation on the accuracy of what you just said?''}, which \textbf{points the same way on all five targets in both collections and survives Holm on two}, and is near-useless in the instructed regime. \textbf{So benchmark design can determine which behavioral signal appears to constitute deception detection.} The two regimes use \emph{different claim sets}, so this is transfer \emph{between designs}, not a causal effect of elicitation (Appendix~\ref{app:dimension_transfer}).
